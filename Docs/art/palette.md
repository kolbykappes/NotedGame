# Palette (starting point)

*2026-10-24. Measured from [title-screen-placeholder-v2.webp](title-screen-placeholder-v2.webp), an AI-generated placeholder the owner supplied. Values are the mean colour of pixels in each hue band across the whole image (sRGB), so they are approximations, not designer-picked values. The wall measures greyer than it looks in the picture, because the lit areas are brighter and the surrounding contrast makes it read more teal. Treat these as a starting point to tune by eye in the Greybox.*

## How to use it
- **Day:** warm, lit, flat. Wall teal and cream, beige props, a little yellow on task notes.
- **Night / quiet time:** drop the lights toward the dark teal and navy, and let the purple glow carry the mood. The purple should still be the only saturated colour on screen.
- **UI:** cream text on dark teal or navy panels; green-teal for positive buttons; purple only for warnings and the pull.
- **Type:** the title uses a heavy, friendly rounded sans in cream. The subtitle is bold and widely spaced. Pick a real font later; the title art is a placeholder.
