## Design brief (collected from the owner, 2026-10-20 and 21)
**Premise:** mess up a data center run by a whimsically named evil AI company; the town wins.

**Wants**
- **Simple to build.** The owner's biggest issue with Rack Farm v1.1 was scope.
- **Bright, cute, simple and fun, in the manner of Can 'Em All** (first-person factory, bright futuristic look), and an **incremental**.
- **First person** (stated firmly), with **aiming as the input**; a **hybrid idler with some targeting** (aiming is not the only thing going on, and the player can opt out of it at a cost).
- **One screen or one small space** (liked from the first conversation).
- **Short:** about **4 to 5 hours**, priced around **$5**. A small amount of content is fine (5 weapons and 5 ideas is enough).
- **Losing is allowed;** a loss costs time, not progress (reset to the start of the week, keep upgrades and currency).
- **Progress you can see in the world,** and upgrades you can **feel the next day**.
- **A loop of recycling that pays for upgrades;** **installed MW against an IPO threshold;** **28 days** to bankrupt the company.
- **Cute and scheming creatures,** not plain racks (the dark-cute edge).
- **Difficulty adjustable at any time.**

**Dislikes**
- Complexity and many systems.
- Too many units on screen; upgrades that are "number go up" with no feel; a game that gives very little feedback.
- Pixel-art zombie looks; the BEAM Inc. style.
- Fixating on one comparison (the owner asked me to stop citing Hardspace's "+10%" upgrades). Content depth is not the concern: Nodebuster-style games have very little content.

**Rejected directions so far:** Unharvest-style spread games; Night Shift roguelite variants; the long lists of non-incremental genres (raccoon sandbox, Rube Goldberg, Ace Attorney and so on); the time loop.

## Scope ladder for the Rack Farm idea
Each rung removes systems from the one above, so the owner can pick the build size.

| Rung | What it is | What it cuts from v1.1 |
|---|---|---|
| **3 (v1.1, parked)** | Walking creatures, plugged state, five weapons, five tricks, machines, defenses, difficulty, town diorama | nothing |
| **2** | Creatures arrive on lanes, one plugged state with a single special ability (a water shot), three weapons, two defenses | walking AI, room systems, five weapons to three |
| **1** | **Static first-person station at an assembly belt:** creatures ride the belt to the plug gate; one tool with upgrade tiers, one special ability, recycling chute, IPO meter, 28 days | pathing, weapon swapping, tricks, room systems, most defenses |
| **0** | **Can 'Em All with a story:** creatures delivered into an arena, kill, crush, recycle, upgrade, a day counter against an IPO meter | everything except the base loop |
