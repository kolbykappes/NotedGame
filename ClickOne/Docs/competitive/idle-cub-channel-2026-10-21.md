## What stands out for ClickOne [inference]
- **Starfall Samurai is the closest thing here to the owner's "hybrid with some targeting"**: a single aimed gesture (drag, release) is the whole verb, in space, with incremental upgrades. Demo likely playable now; nothing about length yet.
- **Fantasy or space with auto-fighting plus a hand verb:** Legion Breaker (battlemage, active incremental, $5.59, released Oct 13; reviews only 77%), Sea Beast Odyssey, My Own Hero Party (**stamina-timed expeditions**, echoing Bills Must Be Paid), Loop Dungeon (**heroes auto-fight, skills need your input**; 65% positive so far).
- **"Mass of enemies" is still a hook:** Slime Break, Comrade, shatAAAAp!, Zombillionaire all sell on huge enemy counts (the owner's Zero Stress King note was the opposite).
- **Reversed or destructive premises get attention:** the most-viewed video is **Unharvest** ("reverse farming"), plus The Big Suck (tornado), What the Buck?! (destroying money). That is the same instinct as the owner's data-center sabotage idea.
- **Very short, very cheap:** One Million Coins ($2.49, 92%) and Rogue Snake ($5.99, 95%, only 22 reviews) show the price band the owner has in mind.
- **Next Fest is the pipeline:** most of the coming-soon games are in the October fest, which ends Oct 26. **Idle Cub's coverage is a marketing channel** for the genre (several videos at 150K to 800K views).

- Only the **first page of the channel** (30 videos) was read; older videos were not.
- Video view counts and ages are as of 2026-10-21. "Reviews" are all-language Steam counts for the full release; demos have separate pages.
- Nothing was watched; descriptions confirmed only the game names and links.
