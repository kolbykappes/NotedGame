## What it means for ClickOne [inference]
- **"Build a data center" is crowded** and mostly sim-style. The one game that matches the owner's **hybrid idler with cursor input** in this theme is **Silicon Sprint**, which has a free demo and launches Nov 2 (worth a look as a comparison on "sweeping cursor" targeting).
- **"Stop the data center"** looks **open**, with a ready-made no-lose frame (the opposition can win without anyone losing) and an obvious comic tone, but it is a **topical, political theme**, which sits far from the owner's current space/fantasy brief. Raised only as an observation.
